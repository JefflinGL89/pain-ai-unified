01 · RESEARCH FOUNDATION

Related Work & Multimodal Novelty

The Clinical Problem

Post-surgical and intensive-care patients frequently cannot verbally communicate acute pain due to sedation or endotracheal intubation. Wired electrodes cause dermal irritation and pressure sores.

Single-modality facial AI is easily confounded by talking or smiling, while vital signs alone can fluctuate due to fever or medication. Fusing both modalities eliminates these failure modes.

RESEARCH QUESTION

Can a contactless, multimodal deep-learning architecture fusing facial expression regression with remote optical photoplethysmography provide robust, real-time clinical pain assessment under unconstrained conditions?

04 · EXPERIMENTAL JOURNEY

What Was Explored & Validated

01

Frame-level ResNet Baseline

Established discrete 4-class baseline with Focal Loss.

02

Overcoming Class Imbalance

Balanced UNBC dataset 1:1 and shifted from discrete classification to continuous regression.

03

ConvNeXt-Tiny + Spatial Attention

Added spatial gating to emphasize brow (AU4), eye (AU6/7), and levator (AU9/10) contractions.

04

Contactless rPPG Integration

Built POS algorithm pipeline with zero-padded FFT and parabolic peak interpolation for sub-BPM precision.

05

Subject-Independent Verification

Eliminated random-split data leakage, proving cross-subject generalization on 4,021 unseen frames.

06

Quality-Gated Multimodal Fusion

Fuses somatic facial grimaces with autonomic heart rate stress and respiratory sinus arrhythmia.

05 · FAILURE ANALYSIS

Addressed Clinical Failure Modes

01

Expression Ambiguity

Talking or smiling triggers facial twitches. Multimodal fusion cross-checks physiological vitals to prevent false alarms.

02

Head Motion & Jitter

Motion penalty gates rPPG weighting to 0% during severe movement, relying temporarily on facial features.

03

Skin Tone & Melanin Bias

POS temporal mean normalization ($C / \mu(C)$) eliminates static melanin pigmentation differences.

04

Lighting Shadows

CLAHE equalization in LAB color space ensures wrinkle contrast remains consistent under indoor clinical lighting.
