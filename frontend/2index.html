01 · RESEARCH FOUNDATION

Related Work & Novelty

The practical problem

Controlled laboratory pain datasets are cleaner than genuine clinical video. Real procedures can introduce occlusion, movement, illumination changes and camera-angle variation.

The motivating clinical work reported approximately 45% accuracy on its clinical data, illustrating the difficulty of robust deployment.

RESEARCH QUESTION

Can an occlusion-aware, multimodal deep-learning architecture combining facial dynamics, remote physiological signals and continuous-time temporal modelling improve robust contactless pain assessment under realistic clinical video conditions?

01

Spatial + Temporal

Combine facial appearance with changes across consecutive frames.

02

Continuous Dynamics

Investigate LNN/LTC-style temporal modelling.

03

Multimodal Extension

Extend toward physiological signals such as remote PPG using BioVid when available.

04

Transparent Evaluation

Report imbalance, confusion, subject variation, confidence and ordinal errors.

05 · FAILURE ANALYSIS

What Went Wrong?

01

Class imbalance

Overall accuracy can hide poor minority-class performance.

02

No Pain vs Low Pain

Subtle expressions create difficult neighbouring class boundaries.

03

Single-frame limitation

One image cannot represent how an expression evolves.

04

Subject variability

Performance can differ substantially between individuals.

05

Complexity ≠ improvement

More sophisticated models do not automatically solve imbalance.

06

Clinical domain gap

Real environments add occlusion, motion and illumination changes.

10 · LIMITATIONS

What This System Does Not Claim

Current live inference is a research demonstration, not clinical validation.

The current ResNet18 checkpoint is a four-class classifier and does not itself predict PSPI.

BioVid physiological fusion and LNN/LTC deployment require completed trained checkpoints.

Model outputs must not replace professional clinical pain assessment.
